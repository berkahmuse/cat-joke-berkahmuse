Entry for [The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands](https://make.fun/c/u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one original single-line joke about cats to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My cat sat on my laptop during my job interview, and the interviewer said, 'He seems qualified' — so now my cat has my job and I have his nap schedule.

151 characters, one line. The setup is an ordinary video-call mishap (cat on the laptop); the turn is the interviewer hiring the cat on the spot, and the sting lands in the last beat — the human keeps the cat's nap schedule, not the job.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
